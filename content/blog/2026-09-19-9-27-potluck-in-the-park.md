Come celebrate Beacon Hill Solidarity's 1st Birthday! We invite you all to our **next potluck, this Sunday 9/27 3pm-6pm at Jefferson Park**. We will share food and relax together in the park. We'll have some optional activities and games. **We will also host a brief "Asks and Offers" section where neighbors can share what support or supplies they need or have to offer to others.** The potluck will be on, rain or shine with canopies. **Kid friendly!**

**Beacon Hill Community Potluck - Games, Activities and Neighborly Connections**

* **Potluck - 3pm - 6pm (flexible end time)**
* **Field games, "Asks and Offers", Spanish conversation circle, neighborhood updates**

As always, feel free to drop in whenever, stay however long, and bring a friend or neighbor.

**When:** Sunday 9/27

**Where:** Jefferson Park - **meet by the skateparkWhat to bring:** Food and drinks, games, picnic blanket, chair, requests/offers for your neighbors

**Access notes:** Jefferson Park is located off of the 36 bus line primarily, about 3/4 mile from the Beacon Hill Light Rail Station. Parking is located near the park on the east side. There is a paved sidewalk around the park and then grass and turf from there. No immediate stairs where we will be located.
