Join Beacon Hill Solidarity on **October 17th** for our **Spooky Season potluck!** We’ll have games, pumpkins to paint, a costume contest (no pressure!), and food to share!   \
\
**Curious about how to organize a union at your workplace?** We’ll have a guest from[ Seattle IWW](https://seattleiww.org/) give a presentation on what a solidarity union is and how to start one at your job.   \
\
As always, feel free to drop in whenever, stay however long, and bring a friend or neighbor. Additionally, our potlucks are always kid friendly. Please bring your kids!\
\
Get to know your neighbors and your neighborhood union—See you there! 

**When:** Saturday, 10/17, 12 PM - 3 PM (Potluck at 12, talk at 1:30, Costume contest at 2:15) \
**Where:** 1607 S. Forest St.\
**What to bring:**

* Halloween and Fall festive food or drinks
* Games
* Requests/offers for your neighbors
* Bring drinks! Especially hot drinks!
* **Bring your winter gourds and pumpkins to decorate!**

\
**Access notes:** The entrance has stairs! Please contact us if you need support with using the stairs.
