Beacon Hill Solidarity is hosting our third **community potluck this coming Saturday, January 17th**! We also have potluck dates scheduled for February and March. See below for details!

**When:** Saturday, January 17th, 12:30-3:30pm
**Where**: 1607 S Forest St
**Access Note**: Stairs to inside of building (no ramp); please contact us if mobility support needed
**What to bring:** A dish, snack, or beverage you'd like to share - or just yourself!


***Other optional things to bring***

1. We are collecting **winter clothes** in good condition to distribute to our neighbors. If you have winter clothes that you would like to donate, please bring them to the potluck.

2. If you have **sealable food containers** for taking leftovers, please bring some for yourself and your neighbors. At the end of our past two potlucks, there was a significant amount of leftover food, so we would love to send folks home with the extras.
