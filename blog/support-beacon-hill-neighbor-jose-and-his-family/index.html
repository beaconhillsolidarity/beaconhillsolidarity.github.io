Support Beacon Hill Neighbor José and his family

March 31, 2026

As many of you may know, ICE kidnapped one of our Beacon Hill neighbors, José, who runs the El Saborcito taco truck, located in the parking lot of the Dino Mart gas station at Beacon Ave S and S Steven St. José is being detained in Texas. As of 1/29, his court hearing was canceled because they have already filed the necessary paperwork with the federal judge. Now we are just waiting for the judge to issue the order for his release, and José will hopefully soon return to his family and Beacon Hill.

More information about José's situation can be found in the flyer at the end of this email. Please help share and donate to this existing fundraiser, which will help cover legal and family expenses.
