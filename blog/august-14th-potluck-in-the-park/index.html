August 14th: Potluck in the Park

August 3, 2026

Come meet your neighbors and share food at our next potluck. We will have games, banner making and other activities. We will also facilitate a brief "asks and offers" space where neighbors can make requests (asks) for needs (help with something, need items) or where neighbors can provide support (offers) when they have an abundance of something (too many figs in their yard or amble time to drive someone to an appointment).
