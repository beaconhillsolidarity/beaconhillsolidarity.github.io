Support Your Beacon Hill Neighbor

September 19, 2026

Support Your Neighbor Juan and his family

Cecilia, Juan, and their family are beloved neighbors in Beacon Hill. Since June 12th, Juan has been detained by ICE at the NW Detention Center, leaving Cecilia and their two children without a reliable source of income at home. Their children are students in Beacon Hill schools, and this family is also part of the local Catholic faith community. Right now, they are facing the pain and uncertainty of separation while also trying to keep daily life steady for their kids.

Support for this fundraiser will help Cecilia cover ongoing essentials like groceries, school supplies, and clothing for the growing kids. It will also help with licensing fees so the family can open their food truck and work toward more stability, along with legal expenses as Juan fights to stay with his family. Because these needs continue over time, recurring donations can make a real difference by helping Cecilia plan for regular household costs and the long legal process ahead. One-time donations are also deeply appreciated and will help meet immediate needs.

Donate here: https://www.gofundme.com/f/support-cecilia-juan-and-family
