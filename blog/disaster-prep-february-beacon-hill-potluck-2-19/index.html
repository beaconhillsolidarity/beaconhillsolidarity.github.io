Disaster Prep: February Beacon Hill potluck 2/19

February 12, 2026

The February Beacon Hill Community Potluck is coming up next week - mark your calendars for next Thursday, February 19th, 5:30-8:30pm, at 1607 S Forest St! At this potluck, we'll have a themed focus on neighborhood natural disaster response, including "Build your own go bag: show and tell."

As always, feel free to drop in whenever, stay however long, and bring a friend or neighbor!
