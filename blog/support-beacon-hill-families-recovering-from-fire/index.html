Support Beacon Hill families recovering from fire

January 28, 2026

On Sunday, January 11th 2026, an apartment fire at the Olympic View Manor destroyed/damaged multiple units and impacted many families and residents. A month later, the GoFundMe fundraiser for supporting the impacted families and neighbors has reached about 50% of its goal of $45k. Donations support families with needs including emergency housing, purchasing necessities, financial help with relocating and legal support, and more.

Link to GoFundMe: https://www.gofundme.com/f/help-beacon-hill-families-after-apartment-fire

Please continue helping to circulate the fundraiser and consider donating!
